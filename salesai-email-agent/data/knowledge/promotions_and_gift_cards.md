# ShopiFyX Promotions, Coupon Codes, Discounts, and Digital Gift Cards Policy

## 1. Promotional Coupons & Discount Codes

### Coupon Application Rules
- **One Coupon Per Order**: Only one promotional discount code or coupon can be applied per checkout session. Coupons cannot be stacked or combined with other promotional promo codes unless explicitly specified during special sitewide clearance events.
- **Minimum Order Value (MOV)**: Most promotional coupons require a minimum cart value (calculated after catalog discounts and before shipping fees and taxes). If an applied coupon specifies ₹1,499 MOV, the cart subtotal must meet or exceed ₹1,499.
- **Category & Product Exclusions**: Coupons may exclude specific high-demand, limited-edition, or heavily discounted clearance items.
- **Expiration and Validity**: Promotional coupon codes have fixed expiration dates and specific usage quotas (e.g., valid until midnight or limited to the first 1,000 customers). Expired coupons cannot be re-activated or manually re-applied by customer support or SalesAI.

## 2. Welcome Discounts and Referral Codes
- **First Order Discount (WELCOME10)**: New registered users receive a 10% discount on their first eligible purchase when applying code `WELCOME10` at checkout.
- **Referral Program**: When an existing customer refers a friend, the friend receives a ₹200 discount coupon on their first order above ₹999. Once the friend's order is successfully delivered (and passes the 7-day return period), the referrer receives a ₹200 reward coupon credited to their account.

## 3. ShopiFyX Digital Gift Cards

### Gift Card Purchase & Delivery
- ShopiFyX Digital Gift Cards are available in denominations of **₹500, ₹1,000, ₹2,000, and ₹5,000**.
- Gift cards are delivered instantly to the recipient's registered email address along with a unique **16-digit card code** and a secure **4-digit PIN**.

### Gift Card Redemption Terms
- **Validity Period**: ShopiFyX Gift Cards are valid for **1 year (365 days)** from the date of issuance. Unused balance expires after 1 year and cannot be extended or refunded.
- **Partial Redemption**: If the total order value is less than the gift card balance, the remaining balance stays on the gift card for future purchases.
- **Combination with Coupons**: Gift Cards are treated as a cash equivalent payment method, meaning customers **CAN combine a Gift Card with a promotional coupon code** during checkout.
- **Multiple Gift Cards**: Customers may apply up to 3 gift cards in a single transaction.
- **Non-Refundable & Non-Reloadable**: Gift cards cannot be reloaded, transferred to another account for cash, redeemed for physical cash, or refunded back to a bank account.

---

## 4. Return and Refund Rules for Discounted Orders

### Partial Returns on Promotional Bundles
If an order was placed using a threshold-based discount (e.g., *"Save ₹300 on orders above ₹1,500"*) or a multi-buy offer (e.g., *"Buy 2 Get 1 Free"*):
- If the customer returns a portion of the bundle and the remaining retained items drop below the required threshold (₹1,500), the promotional discount is revoked.
- The refund issued for the returned product will be adjusted by deducting the applied discount amount to reflect the regular price of the retained items.

### Refunds on Orders Paid via Gift Card
- If an order paid entirely using a Gift Card is cancelled or returned, the refund amount is credited back to the **original Gift Card / ShopiFyX Wallet balance** within **24 hours**.
- For split payments (partially paid by Credit Card and partially by Gift Card), the refund is split proportionally: the card portion returns to the bank card (5–7 business days) and the gift card portion returns to the gift card balance (24 hours).

---

## 5. SalesAI Support Guidelines for Discounts & Gift Cards
- SalesAI must **never generate, promise, or invent custom discount codes** for complaining customers unless an official system-issued compensation coupon is authorized.
- SalesAI should clearly explain coupon eligibility, minimum order requirements, and expiration rules to customers experiencing checkout issues.
