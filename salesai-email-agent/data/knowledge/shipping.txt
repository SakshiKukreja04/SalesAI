ShopiFyX Shipping Policy

1. Shipping Locations
ShopiFyX ships across India.

2. Delivery Time
- Metro cities: 3-5 days
- Other cities: 5-7 days
- Remote areas: 7-10 days

3. Shipping Charges
- Free shipping on orders above 999 INR
- 49 INR shipping for orders below 999 INR

4. Order Processing
Orders are processed within 24 hours.

5. Courier Partners
Reliable courier partners are used for delivery.

6. Tracking
Tracking link is shared via email.

7. Delivery Attempts
Courier will attempt delivery 3 times.

8. Delayed Shipping
Customers will be notified in case of delay.

9. Address Issues
Incorrect address may cause delays or cancellation.
