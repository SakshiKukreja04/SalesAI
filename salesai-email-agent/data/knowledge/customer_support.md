## Escalation

If an issue remains unresolved for 48 hours, the customer may request
escalation to a support manager.

## Information Customers Should Provide

Depending on the issue, support may require:

- Order ID
- Registered email address
- Product name
- Payment/transaction details
- Description of the issue
- Product photographs when applicable

## AI Escalation Rules

SalesAI should recommend human support when:

- The knowledge base does not contain the required information.
- A customer requests an exception to a policy.
- A refund or replacement requires manual verification.
- A payment transaction requires investigation.
- A warranty claim requires product verification.
- The customer reports a serious unresolved issue.
- The customer's issue remains unresolved beyond the stated escalation
  period.
