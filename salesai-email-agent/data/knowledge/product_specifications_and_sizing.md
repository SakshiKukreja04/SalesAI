ShopiFyX follows standard Indian and International sizing standards for all apparel, activewear, and footwear categories. To minimize sizing discrepancies and return requests, customers should refer to the precise measurements detailed below before placing an order.

### Sizing Tips for Apparel
- **Slim Fit vs. Relaxed Fit**: Slim-fit items (e.g., UrbanFit Cotton T-Shirt) have a tailored taper around the waist. Customers who prefer a relaxed or looser fit are advised to order **one size up**.
- **Jackets & Outerwear**: Parkas and winter jackets are designed with extra interior ease (1.5 to 2 inches) to accommodate inner layering (such as thermal wear or sweaters). Order true to your standard chest size.

## 4. Measurement Guidelines: How to Measure Accurately

1. **Chest Measurement**:
   - Wrap a flexible measuring tape horizontally around the fullest part of your chest, directly under the armpits.
   - Keep the tape snug but not tight, ensuring it remains parallel to the floor.
2. **Waist Measurement**:
   - Measure around your natural waistline, located approximately 1 inch above the navel.
   - Do not suck in your breath; maintain a relaxed posture.
3. **Foot Length Measurement**:
   - Place a piece of paper on the floor against a flat wall.
   - Stand firmly on the paper with your heel touching the wall.
   - Mark the tip of your longest toe with a pencil and measure the distance from the edge to the mark in centimeters.

## 5. Material Specifications and Care Instructions

### Combed Cotton Apparel (UrbanFit Collection)
- **Fabric**: 100% Ring-Spun Bio-Washed Combed Cotton (180 GSM).
- **Washing Instructions**: Machine wash cold (below 30°C) with similar colors inside-out. Do not bleach. Tumble dry low or air dry in shade. Warm iron inside-out; do not iron directly on prints or embroidery.

### Footwear Materials (AeroRun Sport Series)
- **Upper**: Multi-layered breathable engineered mesh with TPU welded overlays.
- **Midsole**: High-rebound EVA cushioning foam.
- **Outsole**: Anti-skid carbon rubber compound.
- **Care**: Wipe clean with a damp cloth and mild soapy water. Air dry naturally away from direct heaters or intense sunlight. Do not machine wash.

### Leather Accessories (Wallets & Bags)
- **Material**: 100% Genuine Top-Grain Buff Leather with RFID-blocking alloy lining.
- **Care**: Avoid exposure to water, oil, and harsh chemicals. Polish occasionally with natural leather conditioner to maintain sheen.
