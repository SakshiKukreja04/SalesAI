ShopiFyX Warranty Policy+

1. Warranty Coverage
Selected electronics and accessories come with warranty.

2. Warranty Period
- Electronics: 6 months to 1 year
- Accessories: 3 to 6 months

3. Warranty Includes
- Manufacturing defects
- Technical malfunction
- Hardware failure

4. Warranty Excludes
- Physical damage
- Water damage
- Misuse
- Unauthorized repair

5. Warranty Claim Process
- Contact support
- Provide order ID
- Provide product images
- Verification
- Replacement or repair

6. Warranty Replacement
Replacement provided if product cannot be repaired.

7. Warranty Service Time
5-10 business days.

8. Warranty Proof
Invoice is required for warranty claim.
