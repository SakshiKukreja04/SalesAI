Customer Support Rules:

1. Always acknowledge the customer's concern clearly.
2. If the customer expresses frustration, begin with an apology.
3. Provide accurate information based on company policies.
4. Offer solutions such as tracking updates, refunds, or replacements when applicable.
5. Do not make false commitments or unrealistic promises.
6. Maintain a polite and professional tone at all times.
7. Escalate critical issues such as lost orders or damaged products.
8. Ensure responses are clear, concise, and actionable.
