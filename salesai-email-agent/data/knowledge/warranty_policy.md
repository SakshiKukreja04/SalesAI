# ShopiFyX Warranty Policy

## Warranty Availability

Selected electronics and accessories may include a manufacturer's or
ShopiFyX-supported warranty.

Warranty availability depends on the specific product.

## Warranty Period

Current general warranty ranges are:

- Electronics: 6 months to 1 year
- Accessories: 3 to 6 months

The exact warranty period must be determined from the applicable
product/order information.

SalesAI must not assume that every electronic product has a 1-year
warranty.

## Warranty Coverage

Warranty may cover:

- Manufacturing defects
- Technical malfunction
- Hardware failure

## Warranty Exclusions

Warranty generally does not cover:

- Physical damage
- Water damage
- Misuse
- Unauthorized repair or modification

## Warranty Claim Process

A customer requesting warranty support should:

1. Contact ShopiFyX support.
2. Provide the order ID.
3. Provide product images when requested.
4. Complete the verification process.
5. Follow the repair or replacement instructions.

## Warranty Resolution

Depending on the verification result and product condition, the product
may be repaired or replaced.

Replacement may be provided when the product cannot be repaired.

## Warranty Service Time

Warranty service generally takes 5–10 business days.

Actual resolution time may vary depending on verification, product
availability, and service requirements.

## Warranty Documentation

The purchase invoice is required for warranty claims.

## AI Response Rules

SalesAI must not confirm warranty eligibility without sufficient product
information.

If the exact warranty period is unknown, SalesAI should state the
general policy and ask the customer to contact support or provide the
product/order information.
