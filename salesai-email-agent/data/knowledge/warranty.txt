Warranty Policy:

1. All products come with a standard warranty of 12 months from the date of delivery.
2. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware malfunctions.
3. Warranty does not cover physical damage, misuse, or unauthorized repairs.
4. Customers must provide proof of purchase to claim warranty services.
5. In case of defects, customers can request repair or replacement.
6. Warranty claims are processed within 5-7 business days after verification.
7. For urgent cases, support may prioritize warranty handling.
