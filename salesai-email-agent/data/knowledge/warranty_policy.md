# ShopiFyX Warranty Policy

## Warranty Availability

Selected electronics, footwear, and accessories include a manufacturer's or ShopiFyX-supported warranty.

Warranty availability and terms depend on the specific product category.

## Warranty Period by Category

Current general warranty ranges are:

- **Electronics**: 6 months to 1 year (e.g., smartwatches, wireless earbuds, audio devices).
- **Footwear (Shoes & Sneakers)**: 90 days (3 months) limited warranty against manufacturing defects (e.g., AeroRun Sports Shoes).
- **Accessories**: 3 to 6 months (e.g., leather wallets, belts).
- **Apparel**: 30 days limited warranty covering stitching and zipper manufacturing defects.

The exact warranty period must be determined from the applicable product and order information. SalesAI must not assume that every product has a 1-year warranty.

---

## Footwear Warranty Coverage & Terms

ShopiFyX provides a **90-day (3-month)** limited warranty for all footwear (sports shoes, running shoes, sneakers, and casual shoes) starting from the date of confirmed delivery.

### What is Covered for Footwear:
- **Sole Separation & Delamination**: Outsole or midsole completely or partially detaching from the upper shoe body.
- **Midsole Failure**: Premature midsole cracking, cushioning collapse, or structural defect under normal walking or running usage.
- **Stitching Failure**: Seam bursts, structural stitching unraveling along the upper, heel counter, or toe cap.
- **Eyelet & Hardware Defect**: Broken lace loops, cracked plastic eyelets, or hardware detachment upon normal lacing.
- **Factory Bonding & Adhesive Defects**: Glue failure or manufacturing assembly defects.

### What is Not Covered for Footwear:
- Normal wear and tear, including standard friction wear of the bottom rubber outsole tread.
- Cuts, tears, scuffs, abrasions, or puncture marks caused by sharp objects, pets, or external impact.
- Damage resulting from machine washing, tumble drying, bleach, corrosive detergents, or direct exposure to heaters/open flame.
- Water damage or internal moisture rot on non-waterproof models.
- Sizing mismatches, pinch points, or fit discomfort reported after the standard 7-day exchange window (size issues must be reported within 7 days for a doorstep exchange).

### Footwear Warranty Claim Process:
1. Customer submits their Order ID along with clear photographs:
   - Close-up photo showing the defect (e.g., sole detachment, torn seam).
   - Photo showing the overall top and bottom condition of both shoes.
   - Photo of the inner size/batch label on the shoe tongue.
2. Support team reviews the photos within 24–48 hours.
3. Upon approval, a free reverse courier pickup is arranged.
4. Once verified at the facility, a brand new replacement pair is dispatched immediately (delivery within 3–5 business days). If the customer's size is out of stock, a full refund or store credit is issued.

## General Warranty Coverage

Warranty covers:

- Manufacturing defects
- Technical malfunction
- Hardware failure
- Structural integrity failure under standard usage conditions

---

## General Warranty Exclusions

Warranty does not cover:

- Accidental physical damage, drops, or crushed items
- Liquid intrusion or water damage on non-water-resistant models
- Customer misuse, negligence, or abuse
- Unauthorized modification, third-party repair, or alteration
- Products where the original serial number, IMEI, or batch label has been removed or defaced

---

## Warranty Claim Process

A customer requesting warranty support should:

1. Contact ShopiFyX support or reply directly to their order confirmation email.
2. Provide the Order ID and product name.
3. Provide clear photographs or a short video demonstrating the defect.
4. Complete the verification process with the support team.
5. Follow the instructions for free reverse pickup, repair, or replacement.

---

## Warranty Resolution

Depending on the verification result and product condition:
- The product may be repaired by an authorized service technician, OR
- A brand-new replacement unit may be dispatched if repair is not viable or if the defect is confirmed dead-on-arrival (DOA).
- If the exact model or variant is discontinued/out of stock, a full refund or equivalent store credit is issued.

---

## Warranty Service Timelines

- Initial claim review and photo verification: **24 to 48 hours**.
- Reverse pickup: **2 to 4 business days**.
- Repair or replacement dispatch: **5 to 10 business days** from facility receipt.

---

## Warranty Documentation

The digital order invoice or Order ID is required for all warranty claims. Physical paper warranty cards are not required if the order was purchased directly on ShopiFyX.

---

## AI Response Rules

- SalesAI must not confirm warranty approval without customer verification and photographic proof of defect.
- For footwear questions, SalesAI should clearly state the **90-day manufacturing defect warranty**, explain what is covered (sole separation, stitching), and outline the photo verification process.
- If the exact warranty period for a specific non-catalog item is unknown, SalesAI should state the general category policy (e.g., 90 days for shoes, 6-12 months for electronics) and request the customer's Order ID.
