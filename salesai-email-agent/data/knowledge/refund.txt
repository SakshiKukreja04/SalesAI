ShopiFyX Refund Policy

1. Refund Eligibility
Customers can request a refund within 7 days of product delivery.

2. Refund Conditions
- Product must be unused
- Product must be in original packaging
- Invoice must be available
- Product should not be damaged by customer

3. Refund Process
- Customer submits refund request
- Support team verifies request
- Product pickup scheduled
- Refund initiated after inspection

4. Refund Timeline
- Pickup within 2-4 days
- Refund processed within 5-7 business days
- Amount credited to original payment method

5. Non-Refundable Items
- Personal care products
- Opened electronics
- Clearance sale items
- Gift cards

6. Late Refunds
If refund is delayed, contact support with order ID.

7. Partial Refund
Partial refund may apply for damaged or incomplete returns.

8. Refund Mode
Refund will be credited to original payment source or wallet.
