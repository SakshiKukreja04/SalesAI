# ShopiFyX Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods

ShopiFyX currently supports:

- Credit Cards
- Debit Cards
- UPI
- Net Banking
- Cash on Delivery

## Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery is available for selected locations.

Availability may depend on the delivery address and order conditions.

SalesAI must not state that COD is available for every order without
confirmation.

## Payment Security

ShopiFyX uses secure encrypted payment gateways for processing online
payments.

Customers should never share their card PIN, OTP, CVV, password, or
other authentication credentials with customer support or SalesAI.

## Payment Failure

If a payment fails, the customer should first verify whether the amount
was actually deducted from their bank account or payment method.

If the amount was not deducted, the customer may retry the payment using
an available payment method.

If the amount was deducted but the order was not confirmed, the customer
should contact support with the relevant order or transaction details.

## Payment-Deducted but Order Not Confirmed

SalesAI must not promise an immediate refund.

The customer should provide:

- Order ID, if available
- Transaction reference, if available
- Payment method
- Approximate transaction time
- Amount charged

Support can then investigate the transaction.

## AI Security Rule

SalesAI must never request or store:

- Passwords
- OTPs
- Card PINs
- CVVs
- Full banking credentials
