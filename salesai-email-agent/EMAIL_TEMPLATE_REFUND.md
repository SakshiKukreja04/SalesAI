# Professional Email Response Templates

## Template 1: Refund Request - Acknowledgment & Policy

```
Subject: Re: REFUND - Your Request Has Been Received

Dear [Customer Name],

Thank you for reaching out to us regarding your refund request. We appreciate your patience and want to help resolve this matter quickly.

---

## YOUR REFUND ELIGIBILITY

We're happy to assist with refunds for orders that meet the following criteria:

✓ **Timeline:** Within 7 days of receiving your product
✓ **Condition:** Item must be unused and in original packaging
✓ **Documentation:** Valid order/invoice required
✓ **Condition Check:** Product must be undamaged

---

## NEXT STEPS

To process your refund:

1. **Submit Request** → Fill out our refund form with order details
2. **Verification** → Our support team reviews your request
3. **Pickup Scheduled** → We'll arrange item pickup at no cost
4. **Processing** → Refund credited within 5-7 business days

---

## REFUND TIMELINE

| Step | Timeline |
|------|----------|
| Request Submission | Immediate |
| Support Review | 1-2 business days |
| Pickup Arrangement | Next available |
| Fund Credit | 5-7 business days |

---

## HAVE QUESTIONS?

Feel free to reply to this email or contact our support team directly. We're here to help!

Best regards,  
**Customer Support Team**  
ShopifyX Co.  
support@shopifyx.com  
📞 1-800-SHOPIFY

---

*Please note: Refunds are credited to your original payment method.*
```

## Key Formatting Elements Used:

✅ **Clear Subject** - Specific and action-oriented  
✅ **Personal Greeting** - Professional salutation  
✅ **Scannable Sections** - Headers break up content  
✅ **Icons & Emojis** - Visual interest and clarity  
✅ **Tables** - Compare options easily  
✅ **Numbered Lists** - Step-by-step guidance  
✅ **Whitespace** - Prevents wall-of-text look  
✅ **Strong Call-to-Action** - Clear next steps  
✅ **Professional Signature** - Complete contact info
