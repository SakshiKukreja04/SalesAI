Product Details:

The platform is an AI-powered sales intelligence system designed to assist businesses in automating customer engagement and sales processes.

Key Features:
1. Real-time customer intent detection based on behavioral signals.
2. Emotion analysis to understand customer sentiment.
3. Automated response generation for personalized communication.
4. Multi-agent architecture for decision-making and task execution.
5. Integration with email, chat, and campaign systems.

Use Cases:
- Customer support automation
- Sales engagement optimization
- Lead qualification and follow-up automation

Benefits:
- Reduces manual sales effort
- Improves response time
- Enhances customer engagement
