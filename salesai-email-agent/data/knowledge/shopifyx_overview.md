# ShopiFyX — Company Overview

## About ShopiFyX

ShopiFyX is an online shopping platform serving customers across India.
The platform offers products across apparel, footwear, electronics,
accessories, bags, fitness, home and kitchen, personal care, automotive,
and lifestyle categories.

ShopiFyX focuses on providing affordable products with convenient online
ordering, multiple payment options, delivery tracking, returns, refunds,
exchanges, and customer support.
