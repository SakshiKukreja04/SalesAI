# ShopiFyX Returns, Exchanges, and Replacement Policy

## 1. Return & Exchange Policy Overview
ShopiFyX is committed to customer satisfaction. If an eligible item does not meet your expectations, fits incorrectly, or arrives damaged, customers may initiate a return or exchange request within **7 calendar days of delivery**.

---

## 2. Eligibility & Product Conditions for Returns
To ensure seamless return processing, items must meet the standard Return Quality Check (QC) criteria:
- **Condition**: Unused, unworn, unwashed, and undamaged by customer handling.
- **Packaging & Accessories**: The product must be in its original manufacturer box with all warranty cards, user manuals, tags, labels, and protective wrapping intact.
- **Verification**: The original order invoice and barcode labels on packaging must be preserved for courier scanning.

---

## 3. Product Exchanges (Size & Color Replacements)
ShopiFyX offers **Free Doorstep Exchanges** for eligible apparel, activewear, and footwear:
- **Valid Reasons for Exchange**:
  - Size mismatch (e.g., customer ordered Size M but needs Size L).
  - Color or variant exchange (subject to inventory availability).
  - Minor manufacturing aesthetic defect noticed immediately upon unboxing.
- **Exchange Process**:
  1. Customer requests an exchange for the required size/color.
  2. A replacement order is generated automatically in the system.
  3. Our courier delivers the replacement item while simultaneously picking up the original item (**Hand-in-Hand Exchange**) within **3 to 5 business days**.
- **Stock Limitations**: If the requested replacement size is currently out of stock, the customer will be offered a full refund or store credit instead.

---

## 4. Damaged, Defective, or Tampered Deliveries
If a product arrives broken, damaged in transit, or has missing parts:
- **Mandatory Reporting Window**: The issue must be reported within **48 to 72 hours of delivery** for immediate courier investigation.
- **Documentation Required**:
  - Order ID and registered contact details.
  - Clear photographs or unboxing video showing the outer package label, damaged areas of the product, and original box condition.
- **Resolution Options**: After quick review (within 24 hours), ShopiFyX will arrange an immediate priority replacement at zero additional cost or issue a 100% full refund including any shipping charges.

---

## 5. Wrong Item Received
If the customer receives a product different from what was ordered (e.g., incorrect model, wrong size/color sent by warehouse):
- The customer should report the issue with a photo of the received item and the outer parcel invoice label.
- ShopiFyX will dispatch the correct item via Express Shipping within 24 hours and arrange reverse pickup of the wrong item.

---

## 6. Reverse Pickup Logistics & Quality Check (QC)
- **Free Reverse Pickup**: ShopiFyX provides free doorstep pickup for all eligible returns and exchanges across all serviceable pin codes.
- **Pickup Timeline**: Courier pickup is scheduled within **2 to 4 business days** after request approval.
- **Pickup Attempts**: The courier partner will make up to **2 pickup attempts**. If the customer is unavailable for both attempts, the return request will be closed and the customer will need to re-open it with support.
- **Doorstep Inspection**: The pickup agent performs a quick visual check of tags and basic condition. Final comprehensive QC occurs upon arrival at the central warehouse.

---

## 7. Non-Returnable / Non-Exchangeable Categories
The following items cannot be returned or exchanged due to safety and hygiene:
- Innerwear, shapewear, socks, and lingerie.
- Personal care, grooming kits, and fragrances.
- Final Clearance / Flash Sale items marked *"Non-Returnable"*.
- Opened software, games, and digital gift cards.

---

## 8. AI & Support Guidelines
- SalesAI must explain the step-by-step 7-day policy clearly and help customers initiate size exchanges or refund requests.
- SalesAI must never promise that a non-returnable hygiene item is returnable.
- For damaged items, SalesAI must politely request photographic proof and order ID to expedite the escalation.
