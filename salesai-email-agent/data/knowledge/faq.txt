Q: What is ShopiFyX?
A: ShopiFyX is an online shopping platform offering apparel, electronics, accessories, home products, and lifestyle items at affordable prices.

Q: How can I place an order?
A: You can place an order by selecting a product, adding it to cart, and completing checkout with your address and payment details.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: ShopiFyX accepts Credit Cards, Debit Cards, UPI, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery.

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Yes, orders can be cancelled before shipment from the My Orders section.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: You can track your order using the tracking link sent via email or from the My Orders dashboard.

Q: Do you provide Cash on Delivery?
A: Yes, Cash on Delivery is available for selected locations.

Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days depending on location.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Currently ShopiFyX ships only within India.

Q: Can I change my delivery address?
A: Yes, address can be updated before order is shipped.

Q: What if I receive a damaged product?
A: You can request a replacement or refund within 7 days of delivery.

Q: Are products genuine?
A: Yes, all products are verified and quality checked.

Q: Do you provide discounts?
A: Yes, seasonal discounts and offers are available regularly.

Q: How can I contact support?
A: You can contact support via email or live chat.

Q: Can I return a product?
A: Yes, products can be returned within the return policy guidelines.

Q: Is my payment secure?
A: Yes, ShopiFyX uses secure encrypted payment gateways.

Q: Do you offer bulk orders?
A: Yes, bulk orders are available for selected products.

Q: Can I exchange a product?
A: Yes, exchanges are allowed for size or defective items.

Q: What happens if delivery fails?
A: Our courier partner will reattempt delivery.

Q: Do you provide invoices?
A: Yes, invoices are sent via email after order confirmation.

Q: Can I reorder a product?
A: Yes, reorder option is available in order history.
