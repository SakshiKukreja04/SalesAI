Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

Q1. How can I track my order?
Customers can track their order using the tracking link provided after dispatch.

Q2. What should I do if my order is delayed?
Customers should contact support for updated delivery status and assistance.

Q3. Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be canceled before dispatch. After dispatch, cancellation may not be possible.

Q4. How do I request a refund?
Customers can request a refund through the support team with order details.

Q5. Do you offer warranty on products?
Yes, all products come with a standard 12-month warranty.

Q6. How can I contact customer support?
Customers can reach out via email or chat support for assistance.
