# ShopiFyX Shipping, Delivery Timelines, and Tracking Policy

## 1. Shipping Coverage & Destinations
ShopiFyX operates nationwide delivery across more than 19,000 postal pin codes across India through Tier-1 logistics partners (BlueDart, Delhivery, Ecom Express, Xpressbees, Shadowfax). 
- *Note*: International shipping outside India is currently not supported.

---

## 2. Order Processing & Dispatch Timelines
- **Processing Window**: All confirmed orders are processed, quality-inspected, and packaged within **24 to 48 hours** (business days).
- **Cut-Off Times**: Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on business days are dispatched on the same day whenever possible.
- **Dispatch Notifications**: Once dispatched, the customer receives an automated SMS and Email with the Courier Partner Name, Tracking Number (AWB), and live tracking URL.

---

## 3. Estimated Delivery Timelines by Region

Delivery times are calculated from the date of package dispatch:

| Destination Zone | Estimated Delivery Timeline | Coverage Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Metro Cities** | **3 to 5 business days** | Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad |
| **Tier 2 & Tier 3 Cities** | **5 to 7 business days** | State capitals, major district hubs, and regional commercial centers |
| **Remote & Special Areas** | **7 to 10 business days** | North-East states, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep |

*Delivery Variability*: Timelines represent business days (excluding national holidays, Sundays, and regional strikes). Adverse weather conditions or courier transit delays may occasionally extend delivery times.

---

## 4. Shipping Charges & Free Shipping Threshold
- **Free Shipping**: Available on all prepaid and Cash on Delivery orders with a cart total of **₹999 or above** (after applying all discounts).
- **Standard Flat Shipping Fee**: A nominal flat fee of **₹49** is applied to orders below ₹999 to cover logistics and packaging costs.

---

## 5. Courier Delivery Attempts & Failed Deliveries
- **Delivery Attempts**: Courier partners will make up to **3 delivery attempts** on consecutive business days.
- **Delivery Coordination**: The delivery executive will attempt to call the customer's registered phone number prior to arrival.
- **Return to Origin (RTO)**: If delivery is unsuccessful after 3 attempts due to customer unavailability, incorrect phone number, or refused delivery:
  - The parcel is returned to our central warehouse (RTO).
  - For prepaid orders, a full refund (minus ₹49 shipping charge if applicable) is automatically initiated to the source payment method within 5–7 business days once the parcel is received back at the warehouse.

---

## 6. Order Tracking & Live Updates
- Customers can track live transit milestones by visiting the **My Orders** section on the ShopiFyX website/app or by clicking the tracking link sent via SMS/Email.
- AWB tracking numbers typically activate on the courier's public tracking portal within **12 to 24 hours** after dispatch.

---

## 7. Address Changes & Modifications Prior to Dispatch
- Customers may modify their shipping address or contact phone number **only while the order status is "Processing"** (within 2–4 hours of order placement).
- Once the package status changes to *"Dispatched / In-Transit"*, the delivery address cannot be altered due to automated courier routing constraints.

---

## 8. Delayed Delivery Escalation Protocol
If an order exceeds the maximum estimated delivery window (e.g., more than 6 days in a Metro or 8 days in Tier 2):
1. The customer contacts support with their Order ID.
2. SalesAI/Support lodges an immediate **Priority Courier Trace Ticket** with the carrier operations desk.
3. Carrier updates are provided within **24 hours**. If the carrier confirms the package is lost in transit, ShopiFyX will immediately dispatch an expedited replacement order or issue a 100% full refund.

---

## 9. AI Response Rules for Shipping Inquiries
- SalesAI must provide the published regional delivery ranges (3–5 days for Metro, 5–7 days for other cities).
- SalesAI must **never invent courier names or fake delivery dates**.
- SalesAI must advise customers that shipping address edits are only possible before dispatch.
